# Further Horizon

> This project is a prototype and does not represent the final version of the game.
It was developed and designed by a team of seven people within five weeks as part of a learning project.

# Overview

Further Horizon is a first-person sci-fi action puzzle RPG.
The player takes the role of an explorer trying to repair a damaged mothership trapped near a black hole.
To survive and escape, you must travel to three unique planets — Umbra, Cryovista, and Singara — each offering distinct environments, mechanics, and challenges.
Your mission is to gather essential parts, solve puzzles, and overcome environmental hazards and enemies.

The mothership serves as your central hub and home base, orbiting safely above.
From there, you can select missions, explore planets, and install recovered ship components to unlock new systems and abilities.
The game aims for a tone that is curious, adventurous, and respectful toward all worlds and life forms.

# Gameplay Loop

1. Start at the mothership and select a planet mission.

2. Deploy to the planet’s base camp.

3. Explore the environment, collect artifacts, and solve puzzles.

4. Retrieve the required part or complete the mission objective.

5. Optionally complete side quests or gather crafting materials.

6. Return to camp and extract back to the mothership.

7. Install the recovered component to repair your ship and unlock the next mission.

# Controls

+ WASD - Move
+ Shift - Sprint
+ Space - Jump
+ F - Interact
+ E - Inventory
+ ESC - Pause
+ Mouse - Look Around

# Features

### Planet Umbra

+ Basic collection mechanics (3 collectible items)

+ Switch-controlled doors

+ Inverted controls puzzle

+ Idle enemies with fixed patrol routes

### Planet Cryovista

+ Item collection (heat suit, crafting materials)

+ Find and equip a heat suit for survival

+ Small crafting system with local workstation

+ Laser reflection puzzle based on heat conduction

### Planet Singara

+ Energy-connection mini-game to open doors

+ Enemies with rotating vision cones and patrols

+ Terminal interaction puzzles

### Mothership Hub

+ Planet selection console

+ Ship upgrade and part installation system
